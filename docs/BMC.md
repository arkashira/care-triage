# Business Model Canvas - Care Triage

## Value Proposition
Care Triage offers a streamlined solution for efficiently matching tickets with the most suitable on-demand specialists. By leveraging advanced algorithms and a robust marketplace API, Care Triage ensures that each ticket is assigned to an agent who possesses the necessary expertise and availability. This not only accelerates resolution times but also enhances customer satisfaction by addressing issues promptly and effectively. The system's ability to notify operations managers when no suitable agent is available further ensures that all tickets receive timely attention, thereby maintaining high service standards.

## Customer Segments
1. **Customer Support Teams**: Teams within organizations responsible for handling customer inquiries and resolving issues. They benefit from faster ticket resolution and improved customer satisfaction.
2. **On-Demand Specialists**: Independent professionals or freelancers who provide specialized services on-demand. They gain access to a steady stream of relevant work opportunities.
3. **Operations Managers**: Individuals responsible for overseeing the operational aspects of customer support. They appreciate the system's ability to keep them informed about unresolved tickets.

## Channels
1. **Web API**: The primary channel through which customer support teams interact with Care Triage. It allows for seamless integration with existing ticketing systems.
2. **Mobile App**: An optional channel for on-demand specialists to receive notifications and manage their assignments on-the-go.
3. **Email Notifications**: Automated emails sent to operations managers when no suitable agent is available for a ticket.

## Revenue Streams
1. **Subscription Fees**: Monthly or annual subscription fees charged to customer support teams based on the number of tickets processed.
2. **Commission on Assignments**: A commission fee taken from each assignment made to on-demand specialists.
3. **Premium Features**: Additional charges for premium features such as priority ticket handling and advanced analytics.

## Cost Structure
1. **Development and Maintenance**: Costs associated with the development, maintenance, and continuous improvement of the Care Triage platform.
2. **Marketplace API Integration**: Costs related to integrating and utilizing the MarketplaceAPI for agent suggestions.
3. **Customer Support**: Costs for providing support to customer support teams and on-demand specialists.
4. **Marketing and Sales**: Expenses incurred in promoting Care Triage and acquiring new customers.

## Key Resources
1. **Technology Stack**: The underlying technology stack including the web API, mobile app, and email notification system.
2. **MarketplaceAPI**: The external API used for suggesting agents based on ticket requirements.
3. **Data and Algorithms**: Advanced algorithms and data models used for efficient ticket-agent matching.
4. **Human Resources**: Skilled developers, customer support personnel, and operations managers.

## Key Activities
1. **Platform Development and Maintenance**: Continuous development and maintenance of the Care Triage platform to ensure optimal performance and functionality.
2. **Agent Matching Algorithm Optimization**: Regular optimization of the algorithm used for matching tickets with suitable agents.
3. **Customer Support and Engagement**: Providing ongoing support to customer support teams and on-demand specialists.
4. **Marketing and Sales Efforts**: Implementing marketing strategies and sales efforts to attract new customers and grow the user base.

## Key Partners
1. **MarketplaceAPI Provider**: The provider of the external API used for agent suggestions. A strong partnership ensures reliable and accurate agent recommendations.
2. **Technology Providers**: Partnerships with technology providers for accessing the latest tools and resources needed for platform development and maintenance.
3. **Marketing and Sales Partners**: Collaborations with marketing and sales partners to expand the reach and visibility of Care Triage.
