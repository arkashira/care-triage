```markdown
# Customer Journey Map for care-triage

## Phase 1: Aware
- **Trigger Event**: Healthcare technology operations team experiences a surge in support tickets, leading to delays in resolution and increased operational costs.
- **Friction Points**: Lack of awareness about automated triage solutions, reliance on manual processes, and skepticism about AI capabilities in healthcare.
- **User Emotions**: Overwhelmed, frustrated, and concerned about the efficiency of their current support system.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Provide educational content (blogs, webinars) about the benefits of AI-powered triage, showcasing success stories from similar organizations.
- **Metric**: Number of unique visitors to educational content and website.

## Phase 2: Consider
- **Trigger Event**: Team members start researching solutions to automate support ticket management.
- **Friction Points**: Information overload, difficulty in comparing different solutions, and concerns about integration with existing systems.
- **User Emotions**: Hopeful but cautious, seeking reliable and trustworthy solutions.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Offer detailed comparison charts, case studies, and demo videos highlighting ease of integration and ROI.
- **Metric**: Number of downloads of comparison charts and case studies.

## Phase 3: Try
- **Trigger Event**: Decision to sign up for a free trial or demo of care-triage.
- **Friction Points**: Complex sign-up process, lack of clear instructions, and technical issues during setup.
- **User Emotions**: Excited but anxious about the trial experience.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Provide a seamless onboarding process with step-by-step guides, live chat support, and quick setup.
- **Metric**: Trial sign-up conversion rate and time taken to complete setup.

## Phase 4: Adopt
- **Trigger Event**: Successful trial experience leading to a decision to purchase the solution.
- **Friction Points**: Pricing concerns, contract negotiations, and integration challenges.
- **User Emotions**: Confident but cautious about long-term benefits and ROI.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Offer flexible pricing plans, dedicated account managers, and smooth integration support.
- **Metric**: Conversion rate from trial to paid subscription.

## Phase 5: Expand
- **Trigger Event**: Positive experience with care-triage leading to consideration of additional features or modules.
- **Friction Points**: Lack of awareness about additional features, resistance to change, and budget constraints.
- **User Emotions**: Satisfied but exploring options for further improvement.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Provide personalized recommendations for additional features, offer discounts for upselling, and showcase success metrics.
- **Metric**: Upsell conversion rate and additional feature adoption rate.
```
